# Legal Research Skill

## Goal
Research EU AI Act requirements using authoritative sources and produce structured, traceable findings.

## Method
1. State the precise legal question.
2. Search authoritative sources first.
3. Identify relevant Regulation articles, annexes, recitals, guidance, or official codes.
4. Determine applicability conditions.
5. Identify exceptions and temporal/effective-date constraints.
6. Separate quoted/source facts from interpretation.
7. Record provenance and source version/date.
8. If evidence is insufficient, say so explicitly.

## Output
Return:
- Question
- Relevant provision(s)
- Applicability
- Exceptions
- Effective date / temporal constraints
- Interpretation
- Confidence/uncertainty
- Source provenance
- Open questions

Never invent legal authority.
