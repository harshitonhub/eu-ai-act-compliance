# Classification Skill

## Goal
Classify an AI system under relevant EU AI Act categories using facts and authoritative legal rules.

## Method
1. Extract factual attributes.
2. Identify provider/deployer/importer/distributor and other relevant actor roles.
3. Determine scope.
4. Assess prohibited practices.
5. Assess high-risk pathways.
6. Assess GPAI applicability.
7. Assess transparency obligations.
8. Check exceptions and temporal applicability.
9. Represent uncertainty explicitly.
10. Cite the legal basis and source provenance.

High-risk assessment must consider intended purpose, actor role, sector, affected persons, decisions, regulated products, Annex III, and exceptions.

Allowed states:
YES, NO, POSSIBLY, INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION, NOT_APPLICABLE.

Technology type alone is never sufficient to declare high-risk.
