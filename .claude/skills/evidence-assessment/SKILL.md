# Evidence Assessment Skill

## Goal
Determine whether supplied evidence substantively supports compliance claims.

## Dimensions
Assess:
- Relevance
- Completeness
- Specificity
- Currency
- Traceability
- Consistency
- Sufficiency

## Status
- COMPLIANT
- PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
- NON_COMPLIANT
- NOT_APPLICABLE

Look for contradictions across policies, technical documentation, risk assessments, model/system documentation, questionnaires, and other evidence.

Do not treat the existence of a policy as proof of implementation.
