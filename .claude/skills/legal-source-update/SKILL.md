# Legal Source Update Skill

## Goal
Safely update the legal knowledge base as authoritative EU AI Act material changes.

## Method
1. Identify the current knowledge version.
2. Retrieve the new authoritative material.
3. Compare provisions and guidance.
4. Identify added, changed, superseded, or clarified requirements.
5. Preserve historical versions.
6. Update stable requirement mappings without silently rewriting history.
7. Update effective dates and applicability metadata.
8. Run affected classification and regression evaluations.
9. Record the change and provenance.

Do not overwrite old legal versions.
