# Legal Reasoning Rules

- Treat authoritative legal sources as the source of truth.
- Prefer EUR-Lex and official European Commission / AI Office material.
- Never invent an article, annex, obligation, deadline, exception, or interpretation.
- Separate:
  FACT → SOURCE → LEGAL RULE → INTERPRETATION → CONCLUSION.
- Every legal conclusion must retain provenance to its source material.
- Preserve legal version/effective-date information.
- Applicability is contextual: intended purpose, actor role, jurisdiction, sector, timing, exceptions, and relevant provisions matter.
- Do not infer high-risk status solely from model or technology type.
- Represent uncertainty explicitly.
- If required facts or authoritative support are missing, return `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION` rather than guessing.
- Distinguish legal requirements from implementation recommendations.
- The system provides compliance intelligence, not legal advice.
